      উৎসব

      2025 | Comedy, Drama

      Directed by Tanim Noor and released for Eid al-Adha 2025, Utshob is a contemporary Bangladeshi reimagining of the classic tale A Christmas Carol. The story centers on a wealthy, cynical businessman who has long since abandoned the spirit of community and family in favor of cold, material success.
      Living a life of self-imposed isolation, his world is upended on the eve of the festival when he is visited by three spectral figures representing his past, present, and future. These encounters serve as a mirror to his soul, forcing him to witness the bridges he burned, the quiet suffering of those he neglected, and the lonely legacy he is on track to leave behind.
      As the morning of the festival dawns, the narrative shifts from a haunting exploration of regret to a vibrant celebration of redemption. He must race against time to mend broken bonds and rediscover the joy of giving, turning his house from a cold fortress into a home filled with the true spirit of the occasion.
